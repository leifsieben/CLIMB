### Fig A1.c · overall standing across all four benchmark suites (rank summary)

Fig A1.a/b compare the models *within* each MoleculeNet dataset. This pools every benchmark we ran
into a single ranking, across all four suites — **MoleculeNet, Polaris, MoleculeACE, and the CBS
virtual-screen**. The metrics are heterogeneous (RMSE, ROC-AUC, NEF1%, Spearman…), so scores cannot be
averaged — the models are compared by **rank**. To keep the summary from being dominated by the two
large suites (MoleculeACE 30 + Polaris 28 datasets vs MoleculeNet 6 + CBS 1), each suite is weighted
**equally**: rank within a suite, rescale to a common 1–12 scale, then average the four suites. Same 12
models as Fig A1.b. CheMeleon (e2e)'s Polaris/MoleculeACE numbers are Burns et al.'s published values.
