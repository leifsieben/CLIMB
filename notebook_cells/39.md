## SI · The same ladder, measured against the end-to-end baseline

Fig SA measures lift over the **frozen** random encoder. Everywhere else in the paper (I1, B2, C1J1)
lift is measured over **no_pretrain (end-to-end)** — a random encoder *fine-tuned* end-to-end per task,
which is what a practitioner would actually do instead of pretraining. This companion re-plots the same
ladder (and Wikipedia arm) against that honest baseline (`phase2/e2e_random_0*`, native, mean over 3
seeds — the Fig I1 source; e2e is a fine-tuned baseline, so it is not part of the frozen-probe
`_baselines` re-eval).

The composition story is unchanged — **shuffle ≈ real** and **unigram far below** — but two things
sharpen: **QM7 turns slightly negative for every arm** (end-to-end fine-tuning a random encoder already
beats the frozen MLM probe there, so MLM buys nothing on QM7), and the crisp "unigram = 0" reading of
Fig SA softens, because the frozen and end-to-end floors differ per task. HIV = ROC-AUC.
