## External validation · Cystathionine β-Synthase (CBS) inhibitor virtual screening

We ran the full A1.a model battery on an **external** VS benchmark — 43 CBS inhibitors vs 10,402
inactives/decoys (0.4% prevalence) from Truong et al. 2026 (*J. Cheminformatics*) — on the benchmark's
**own provided, leakage-controlled 5-fold split** (UMAP-cluster folds, max inter-fold Tanimoto < 0.70).
Metric = **NEF1%** (normalized enrichment of true actives at top-1%); ROC-AUC is near-ceiling here and
less informative.

The classical **Morgan+descriptor+XGBoost** anchor (`fp_desc`, 0.93) is the single best model — it beats
every pretrained CLM encoder **and** Truong's target-trained, structure-based models (0.764 ± 0.191),
using no docking or 3D structure. MLM pretraining (`unsup_only`, 0.74) does not even beat the frozen
random-init encoder (0.76). The cheap ligand-only baseline wins again — on an external target this time.

Reference overlays are from Truong 2026: their **target-trained** structure-based models
(0.764 ± 0.191, the dashed line + band); their **ligand-only** descriptor baseline and the **16 generic**
docking/co-folding pipelines both sit near 0 (all 16 generic pipelines scored 0.000 on their Fold 3). As
a faithfulness check we reproduced their descriptor baseline at **0.07** inside our pipeline, and
confirmed our folds match their Table 1 exactly with no cross-fold leakage. **Caveat:** faithful to the
published benchmark, so "ligand-only matches/beats structure-based **on this benchmark**" is solid; the
standard retrospective-VS decoy/analogue-bias limitation (Sieg 2019; Chen 2019) means a "prospective CBS
screener" claim is not established — the decoys are physicochemical-property-matched to the actives but
not structure-matched, so structural fingerprints retain a handle the descriptor baseline lacks.
